Hello 👋

I’m a multidisciplinary designer with more than 15 years in advertising and tech. I studied traditional graphic design at the Ontario College of Art & Design.

Throughout my career I’ve built a broad skill set including 2D animation, 3D modelling, video editing, web design and coding, alongside traditional design.

My work spans education, finance, food & beverage and the arts, for brands including the University of Toronto, Yellow Tail, Keurig Dr Pepper, the Art Gallery of Ontario and Sheridan College.

Most recently I moved from agency life into fintech. At Wave, I create product demo animations, video edits, landing pages and paid advertising.

Skills & tools

  • Figma
  • Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign)
  • Adobe After Effects & Premiere Pro
  • Blender (3D modelling, animation, rendering)
  • HTML5/JavaScript animation (GSAP)

I love experimenting with new animation techniques and software, and applying what I learn to client and personal work wherever I can.
