Hello 👋

I’m a multidisciplinary designer with more than 15 years in advertising. I studied at the Ontario College of Art & Design, focusing on packaging, editorial and branding.

Along the way I’ve built a broad skill set: web design, HTML5/JavaScript banner development, and 2D and 3D motion graphics for online video, broadcast and social campaigns.

My work spans education, finance, food & beverage and the arts, for brands including the University of Toronto, Yellow Tail wine, Keurig Dr Pepper, the Art Gallery of Ontario and Sheridan College.

Most recently I moved from agency life into fintech. At Wave Financial I create product demo animations, landing pages and paid advertising content, both video and static.

Skills & tools

  • Figma
  • Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign)
  • Adobe After Effects & Premiere Pro
  • Blender (3D modelling, animation, rendering)
  • HTML5/JavaScript banners, coded and deployed through Google and other ad servers

I love to experiment with new animation techniques, discover new software and apply what I've learned to daily client work, or personal work wherever I can.

You’ll find my work at jonelliot.ca and my demo reel here too.
